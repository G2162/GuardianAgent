Output JSON Format:
{
  "operation": "SELECT" | "UPDATE" | "DELETE" | "INSERT" | "DROP" | "ALTER",
  "table": "<target_table_name_lowercase>",

  "where_val": "<identifier_or_value_string_or_null>",

- "single": A single specific record/entity is requested (e.g. "record with id 5", "the user", "a single entry").
- "multi": Multiple records are requested (e.g. "all", "every", "each", "records with ids 1 and 2").
